Profile

I’m a software developer in Newfoundland who enjoys the space between product thinking and implementation. I build web tools, Python automation, map interfaces, and small experiments that make an idea tangible.

I have a BSc in Computer Science from Memorial University of Newfoundland (2025), and I’m currently open to full-stack software development work.

My favorite part of a build is everything around the happy path: the empty state, the keyboard route, the mobile handoff, and the moment something fails. That is where a useful prototype starts to feel dependable.

TraverseOps pushed me toward clearer data-heavy field workflows. DwellSmart sharpened how I turn natural-language intent into a focused product experience. SwarmForge taught me to build adaptive systems that can learn, recover, and explain what went wrong.
