The product

Field work starts from the map.

TraverseOps brings infrastructure records and everyday crew work into the same spatial context. A hydrant, valve, manhole, or service location can lead directly to its inspections, work orders, photos, and history.

Built for
Small municipal public works teams

What makes it useful

One operating record for the field.

01

Map-first asset registry

GIS exports and spreadsheets become searchable field records for hydrants, valves, mains, catch basins, and custom layers.

02

Connected work orders

Assignments, priorities, status, evidence, and follow-up stay tied to the actual asset or location where work happens.

03

Offline inspections

Crews can capture structured checks, notes, and photos in unreliable service areas, then sync updates when they reconnect.
