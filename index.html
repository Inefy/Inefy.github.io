How I work

Practical by default. Curious by design.

I care about the small decisions that make a product feel understandable: the first action, the empty state, the recovery path, and the last click.

01

Make complexity legible

Turn dense data and multi-step work into layouts people can scan, trust, and act on.

02

Build the working surface

Ship the interface, the useful interaction, and the resilient states around it—not just the happy-path screenshot.

03

Leave the system clearer

Prefer small, maintainable frontends with explicit content, accessible controls, and honest constraints.
